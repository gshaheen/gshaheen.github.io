# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Clean, zero-dependency static HTML5/CSS/JS with custom-crafted typography and canvas. Zero build step, instant GitHub Pages deployment.

## Users

Visitors, leaders, founders, and technical peers seeking a direct, high-signal overview of George Shaheen's side project engineering work.

## Product Purpose

A hyper-minimal personal portfolio that lets the work speak for itself without contrived bios, manifestos, or self-explanations.

## Positioning

Pure editorial minimalism: stark, calm, distraction-free. Replaces verbose portfolio pitches with an ultra light grey canvas, direct project links, and an understated ambient interactive element.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers. Single-mode ultra light grey (`#f4f4f5`) environment with no dark/light mode toggles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-mode ultra light grey background.
- Direct links to LinkedIn and GitHub.
- Clean showcase of pinned side projects: Dispatch, ResourceDeck, Allocations.
- Super simple project entries with very short descriptions.
- Subtle ambient coordinate canvas that quietly responds to pointer physics.
- High contrast and full accessibility (WCAG AAA contrast on primary text, WCAG AA on muted text).

## Brand Commitments

- Owner: George Shaheen
- Domain: `georgeshaheen.com`
- Aesthetic: Hyper-minimal, understated, elegant, calm, single-mode ultra light grey.
- Voice: None required; the work speaks directly.

## Product Principles

1. **Ruthless Subtraction**: Remove everything that does not earn its place. No contrived explanations or self-aggrandizing prose.
2. **Whitespace Over Containers**: Structure the page with typography and vertical rhythm, never with decorative cards or boxes.
3. **Quiet Craft**: An understated, ambient interaction that engages the visitor's curiosity without interrupting their focus.
